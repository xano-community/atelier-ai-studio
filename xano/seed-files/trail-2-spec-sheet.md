# Marlow Trail 2: spec sheet

A hardtail trail bike for rough ground and long days. Price £1,850. Colours: sage green and slate.

## Sizing

The Trail 2 rides long. Between two sizes, take the smaller one.

| Frame | Rider height | Reach | Stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 155 to 168 cm | 445 mm | 610 mm |
| M | 166 to 178 cm | 470 mm | 620 mm |
| L | 176 to 188 cm | 495 mm | 634 mm |
| XL | 186 to 198 cm | 520 mm | 648 mm |

## Frame and fork

- Frame: 6061 aluminium, internal routing, lifetime frame warranty for the first owner.
- Fork: 130 mm air fork with lockout.
- Head angle 65.5°, seat angle 76°.
- Tyre clearance: up to 2.4 in on 29 in wheels.

## Drivetrain and brakes

- 1x12 groupset, 30T chainring, 10–51T cassette.
- Hydraulic disc brakes, 180 mm rotors front and rear.

## Weight and extras

- 13.9 kg (size M, without pedals).
- Dropper post: 150 mm on S and M, 170 mm on L and XL.
- Bottle mounts inside the front triangle and under the down tube.

## Care

Service the fork every 50 hours of riding. The frame warranty covers the frame for as long as the first owner keeps the bike.
